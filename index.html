Principles

  • Own the software you run. Buy it, keep it, self-host it; subscriptions are a business model, not an engineering requirement.
  • Make the smallest change that solves the problem. Grand rewrites are a last resort, not a plan.
  • Prefer boring technology. The interesting part of your product should not be its infrastructure.
  • Decide with evidence, then write it down. A decision without its reasons is a rumour the next team inherits.
  • Ship in small, verified increments. Working software, inspected early, beats a big reveal.
